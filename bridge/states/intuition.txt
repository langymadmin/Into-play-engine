# Intuition — the engine asks the OTHER side a question.
#
#   Search your library for three cards and reveal them. Target opponent
#   chooses one. Put that card into your hand and the rest into your
#   graveyard. Then shuffle.
#
# Chosen because of one word in its script: Chooser$ Targeted. The choice is
# not ours. At a kitchen table the opponent makes it by reaching over to the
# tablet, or by saying it out loud while we tap — so the UI has to be able to
# say "this question is not yours" and still accept the answer on this screen.
# Nothing in UI 2.5 can express that; it is the clearest case for the priority
# band naming who is being asked.
#
# It also exercises, in one cast: a library search with a count (3 of N), a
# reveal, a choice made by another seat, and two different destinations for
# the cards that were and were not chosen.
#
# Library is small and known so the three found cards are predictable in the
# log. Three Islands untapped pays for Intuition (2U) with nothing left over,
# which is deliberate: if mana were spare, a failure to pay would hide inside
# a successful cast.

humanlife=20
humanhand=Intuition
# No |Tapped modifier, and NOT |Tapped:False. GameState tests the modifier with
# info.startsWith("Tapped") and then calls tap() — so "Tapped:False" taps the
# card and silently throws the ":False" away. Untapped is the absence of the
# word. Written out because the failure is invisible: three tapped Islands look
# identical to three untapped ones in the state file, and the only symptom is a
# spell that cannot be paid for.
humanbattlefield=Island;Island;Island
humanlibrary=Llanowar Elves;Grizzly Bears;Shivan Dragon;Counterspell;Island

ailife=20

aibattlefield=
